Create a formal academic lecture scribe based strictly on the attached lecture slides and relevant textbook material. The scribe must serve as reliable reference material for a closed-notes examination, therefore it must prioritize correctness, precision, and strict faithfulness to the provided source material rather than creative explanation.
Content Requirements:
1. Include all definitions, symbols, and mathematical notations introduced in the lecture.
2. Clearly state all assumptions, propositions, theorems, or formal results exactly as presented in the lecture material.
3. Reconstruct all proofs, derivations, and worked examples step by step whenever they appear in the provided context.
4. Do not omit intermediate steps that are necessary to understand the reasoning shown in the lecture.
Restrictions:
1. Do not introduce new examples, explanations, or interpretations that are not present in the provided material.
2. Do not simplify concepts or provide intuitive explanations beyond what is explicitly given in the lecture.
3. If a concept, example, or derivation is not included in the context, do not generate it.
Structure:
1. Organize the scribe following the same logical sequence as the lecture.
2. Use clear section headings and subheadings to maintain the conceptual flow of the lecture.
3. Ensure the final document can be used directly by a student for accurate revision of the lecture content.
